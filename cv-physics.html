Research interests

Light-front physics: light-front quantization, the light-cone Fock-basis expansion of baryons.
Proton structure: collinear PDFs, transverse-momentum distributions and 3D parton distributions, constrained jointly by DIS, SIDIS and Drell–Yan.

Research directions & work in progress

Light-front quantization (PH 01). Kinematics turn multiplicative and the vacuum gets simple, which pushes all the difficulty into the Fock-space truncation. I work on where that truncation can be trusted.

Baryon light-cone Fock expansion (PH 02). Wave functions expanded in the light-cone Fock basis, three-quark sector first; convergence, and which observables survive a cut at fixed N.

Proton structure — DIS / SIDIS / DY (PH 03). Three processes read together: DIS for the collinear PDF, SIDIS for transverse momentum, DY as the cross-check. The 3D distribution only holds once the three agree.

Open questions

Does convergence come from restricting transverse momentum or from the number of Fock components?

How much do DIS, SIDIS and DY actually disagree on the same ⟨kT²⟩?

In which Q² range does a Gaussian width model stop being defensible?
