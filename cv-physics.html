Research interests

I am interested in how much of hadron structure can be derived rather than fitted.
Light-front QCD. Quantise on the light front and the vacuum turns trivial — the difficulty does not disappear, it reappears in the Fock truncation and in the zero modes the A+=0 gauge leaves behind. Next for me: the holographic link from confinement scale to bound-state equation.
Hadron structure from Fock wave functions. A transverse-momentum distribution read as a spectator-integrated amplitude, not a fitting function, with Σxi=1 and Σk⊥i=0 built in from the start.
TMD factorisation. Collins–Soper evolution and its non-perturbative kernel; the SIDIS structure-function dictionary (Sivers, Collins, Boer–Mulders); the Drell–Yan sign reversal that keeps SIDIS and DY from being fitted apart.

Research directions & work in progress

Light-front quantisation (PH 01). Working toward: an answer to where the Fock-space truncation can still be trusted. The two handles I am following are DLCQ matrices whose UV cutoff is matched to the truncation, and what the zero modes keep carrying once the gauge is fixed.

Baryon light-cone Fock expansion (PH 02). Working toward: a truncation checkable against constraints that do not bend — normalisation (Σn∫|ψn|²=1, ∫dx f1 equal to the valence count) and the mass relation P+P−−P⊥2=M2 — three-quark sector first, then five, and which observables survive a cut at fixed N.

Proton structure — DIS / SIDIS / DY (PH 03). Working toward: one picture the three processes agree on. TMD factorisation at PhT≪Q, matched onto a Y-term at large transverse momentum, Collins–Soper evolution with a non-perturbative kernel, and where a Gaussian width in ⟨k⊥²⟩ gives way to power-law tails.

Open questions

PH 01 · Light-front quantization. Does the multiplicative kinematics actually save work, or does it just move the work into the Fock space? · If the vacuum got simpler, which physics got moved into the wave function instead? · Which observable is the right monitor for truncation error — and how would I know, before I have the exact answer?

PH 02 · Baryon light-cone Fock expansion. Is convergence driven by cutting k⊥ or by adding Fock components? · Which observables are least sensitive to the cut — and are they the interesting ones? · In which process do five-quark components rise above the truncation error?

PH 03 · Proton structure — DIS / SIDIS / DY. Do SIDIS and DY disagree on the same ⟨k⊥²⟩ — and is a disagreement a sign reversal or an evolution effect? · In which Q² range does the Gaussian width model start to fail? · Which term of the 3D distribution is shortest on data — and is that a fact about the data, or about the factorisation?
