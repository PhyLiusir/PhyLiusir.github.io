01

Research

Three threads, one question: what the proton is made of, and whether a small model can learn it.

2025 — now

Transverse-momentum dependent distributions

Sivers and Boer–Mulders functions in SIDIS and Drell–Yan. I built a Gaussian model with width² = z²⟨kT²⟩ + ⟨pT²⟩ to extract ⟨kT²⟩ from published multiplicities, then checked the result against JLab 12 GeV data from CLAS12 and Hall C.

TMD · SIDIS · CLAS12 · model fits
2025 — 2026

Spin structure and higher twist

Polarized parton densities, twist-3 contributions and Gegenbauer moments. I am working through Bacchetta (2007) and Avakian (2019) line by line, and turning the notes into a short manuscript for EPJ Plus.

Polarized PDF · higher twist · EPJ Plus
2026 — now

Small models doing physics

A shared-weight loop FFN with SwiGLU beat stacked baselines by +0.43 pp at depth 4 on Higgs. That result produced a depth-aware optimizer family, sign(g)·|g|1/n, shown equivalent to ℓn+1-NSD; I am now testing DEQ-style self-stopping iterations.

PyTorch · architecture ablations · CUDA
