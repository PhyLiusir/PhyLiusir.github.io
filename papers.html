MANUSCRIPT · 2026 EPJ Plus — in preparation

Higher-twist corrections to polarized TMDs from Gegenbauer moments

Chenxu Liu · Shandong University, Qingdao

Twist-3 contributions to polarized transverse-momentum dependent distributions are usually absorbed into an effective Gaussian width. I rewrite the transverse-moment expansion in a Gegenbauer basis and show that the first two moments already carry most of the correction in the moderate-x, low-qT region. The note compares the resulting widths with published SIDIS multiplicities and says plainly what measurement would be needed to separate the expansion from a plain Gaussian fit.

KEY RESULT

Two Gegenbauer moments reproduce the extracted ⟨kT²⟩ inside the current experimental error — the data cannot yet tell a truncated expansion from a wider Gaussian.

PREPRINT · 2026 arXiv — submission planned November 2026

Depth-aware optimizers for weight-tied feed-forward stacks

Chenxu Liu · Shandong University, Qingdao

A weight-tied loop network applies the same block n times, so the gradient that reaches the shared weights is scaled differently from a plain stack. I study the element-wise family sign(g)·|g|1/n and prove it is exactly steepest descent normalised under the ℓn+1 norm. On a Higgs classification task the depth-aware variant stays stable at depths where Adam diverges, and matches a well-tuned baseline at depth 4.

KEY RESULT

sign(g)·|g|1/n is ℓn+1-NSD, not an approximation of it — depth belongs in the optimizer, not only in the architecture.
